# HR-205 — Pune Regional Leave Addendum

> **Training data notice:** This addendum is created for the Week 3 RAG exercise. It is not an official company policy. General rules are based on the supplied Employee Handbook; regional values below are synthetic test data.

## 4.1 Annual Leave Eligibility

Employees in the Pune region follow the company's annual leave process. Leave requests require prior TL/Manager approval and must be submitted through the ERP.

## 4.2 Carry-over Rule

For this training addendum, eligible confirmed employees may carry forward up to **10 leave days** to the next calendar year.

### Eligibility Table

| Employee status | Carry-over cap |
|---|---:|
| Probationary employee | 0 days |
| Confirmed employee | 10 days |
| Employee with 3+ years experience and 1+ year continuous service | 10 days |
