# HR-204 — Sick Leave Addendum

**Policy ID:** HR-204  
**Region:** AMER  
**Effective Date:** 2026-03-01

## 1. Sick Leave Entitlement

Eligible employees receive up to 10 paid sick-leave days per calendar year.

## 2. Medical Documentation

A medical certificate may be requested for absences of more than 3 consecutive working days.

## 3. Notification

Employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible when they cannot report to work because of illness.

## 4. Unused Sick Leave

Unused paid sick leave does not carry over to the following calendar year.

## 5. Scope

This addendum applies to AMER employees.
