# HR-203 — Parental Leave Addendum

**Policy ID:** HR-203  
**Region:** EMEA  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-15

## 1. Primary Parent Leave

An eligible primary parent may receive up to 16 weeks of parental leave.

## 2. Secondary Parent Leave

An eligible secondary parent may receive up to 4 weeks of parental leave.

## 3. Eligibility

Eligibility requires active employment on the leave start date and completion of the applicable probation requirement.

## 4. Notice

Employees should notify Human Resources at least 30 calendar days before the expected leave start date when reasonably possible.

## 5. Scope

This addendum applies to EMEA employees.
