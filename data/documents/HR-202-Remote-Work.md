# HR-202 — Remote Work Policy

**Policy ID:** HR-202  
**Region:** APAC  
**Effective Date:** 2026-02-01

## 1. Eligibility

Employees may request remote work after completing their probation period and meeting role requirements.

## 2. Weekly Limit

Eligible employees may work remotely for up to 2 days per week unless a business unit has an approved exception.

## 3. Manager Approval

Remote-work schedules require manager approval and may be changed based on operational needs.

## 4. Equipment

Company-issued equipment must be used for company work. Employees are responsible for protecting company information when working remotely.

## 5. Scope

This addendum applies to APAC employees.
