# HR-201 — Annual Leave Policy

**Policy ID:** HR-201  
**Region:** APAC  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01

## 1. Annual Leave Entitlement

Employees receive annual leave based on completed years of service.

| Employee group | Completed service | Annual leave |
|---|---:|---:|
| Regular employee | Less than 1 year | 12 days |
| Regular employee | 1 to less than 3 years | 15 days |
| Regular employee | 3 years or more | 18 days |

## 2. Carry-Over

Employees may carry over up to 5 unused annual-leave days into the following calendar year. Carry-over must be used by March 31.

## 3. Approval

Annual leave requests should normally be submitted at least 5 working days before the planned leave date. Manager approval is required.

## 4. Scope

This addendum applies to APAC employees covered by the company leave program.
