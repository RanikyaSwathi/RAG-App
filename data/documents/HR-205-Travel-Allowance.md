# HR-205 — Business Travel Allowance Addendum

**Policy ID:** HR-205  
**Region:** APAC  
**Effective Date:** 2026-02-15

## 1. Eligibility

Employees traveling on approved company business may claim eligible travel expenses.

## 2. Daily Meal Allowance

The standard meal allowance is USD 45 per eligible travel day.

## 3. Hotel Allowance

The standard hotel allowance is USD 150 per night, subject to approved exceptions.

## 4. Expense Submission

Travel expenses must normally be submitted within 15 calendar days after returning from the business trip.

## 5. Scope

This addendum applies to APAC employees.
