# HR-207 — Probation and Eligibility Addendum

**Policy ID:** HR-207  
**Region:** APAC  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-10

## 1. Probation Period

New regular employees normally complete a 6-month probation period.

## 2. Carry-Over Eligibility During Probation

The following table defines the annual-leave carry-over cap based on employee status.

| Employee status | Completed service | Carry-over cap |
|---|---:|---:|
| Probationary employee | Less than 3 months | 0 days |
| Probationary employee | 3 to less than 6 months | 2 days |
| Regular employee | 6 months or more | 5 days |

## 3. Remote Work Eligibility

Employees who have not completed probation are not eligible for the standard remote-work allowance.

## 4. Performance Review

Probation completion is subject to satisfactory performance and manager confirmation.

## 5. Scope

This addendum applies to APAC employees.
